# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Be respectful and constructive. This is a small project — keep interactions
focused on the work.

Specifically:

- Be kind and patient with contributors of all experience levels
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus on what's best for the project

Arguing about the alphabet is encouraged. Arguing about people is not.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reserves the right to remove comments, close issues, or ban
users who behave poorly. This is a hobby project and the bar for tolerance is
low.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, PRs, and discussions.
